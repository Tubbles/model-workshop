# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code working on kbdkid4's printed parts, in `model/kbdkid4/`. The workshop's layout, workflow and conventions are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md).

## What this is

The 3D-printed parts of kbdkid4 (`~/dev/kbdkid4`, https://github.com/Tubbles/kbdkid4), a split wireless keyboard designed in LibrePCB: its tray, switch plate, PCB replica and carrying case, built by FreeCAD scripts in `model/kbdkid4/scripts/` from the board's files. The kbdkid4 repository holds the board design and never reads anything from here.

## Board files

The scripts read kbdkid4's own files, never a copy: locally they are in `~/dev/kbdkid4`, in CI in a checkout of Tubbles/kbdkid4's default branch at `external/kbdkid4`. They open them relative to the working directory, so every export runs with a kbdkid4 checkout as its working directory. `board_step.py` reads `boards/boards.lp`, the default board it lists (currently `boards/board_1.1/board.lp`) and `circuit/circuit.lp`, and every export takes the STEP model as an argument, kbdkid4's committed `output/v1/kbdkid4_v1.step` (the output of the one STEP job in its `project/jobs.lp`). The plate also takes the kbdkid3 plate model as an argument, `resources/kbdkid3-plate-left.FCStd` at this repository's root. The exports never use a STEP regenerated by librepcb-cli, so a board change reaches them once kbdkid4 commits the new STEP. A push to kbdkid4 does not run this repository's CI. A manual run (`workflow_dispatch`) picks up kbdkid4's latest main.

## Tray export

`model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_tray.py` builds a 3D-printable tray STL from the board's STEP model, with bored standoffs for M2 heat-set inserts at the board's mounting drills, running FreeCAD headless in a container (`docker/freecad.Dockerfile`, Debian trixie's FreeCAD 1.0). CI runs each one with the kbdkid4 checkout as the container's working directory and this repository mounted at `/work`:

    docker run --rm -v "$PWD":/work -w /work/external/kbdkid4 freecad-headless \
        freecadcmd /work/model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_tray.py --pass output/v1/kbdkid4_v1.step /work/tray/kbdkid4_v1_Tray_Left.stl [key=value ...] [flip]

Parameters and their defaults live in the constants block at the top of the script (gap, wall, floor, depth and standoff height in `model/kbdkid4/scripts/tray_shape.py`); the usage header lists the key=value override names.

`model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_plate.py` exports the switch plate the same way: the kbdkid3 plate model (`resources/kbdkid3-plate-left.FCStd`, a parametric switch cell replicated by a point array, with a mirrored right plate that is not exported) cut with clearance holes for the screw heads at the board's mounting drills (the screws clamp the PCB, the switches hold the plate). The kbdkid3 plate does not share the kbdkid4 board's origin; the script aligns it by matching the plate's cutout centers against the switch positions parsed from `board.lp` and `circuit.lp`, accepting only a pure translation. The plate then extends over the switch-less microcontroller corner, with frustum recesses on its underside at every lead drill found in the STEP but the nice!view header's five (DS1, not fitted for now), stopping 0.4 mm short of the top face (the through-hole parts sit on the board's underside, their leads stick up), and for the reset switch (pressed with a SIM ejector pin) a 3.0 mm pin hole with a 1.0 mm deep pocket in the underside over the switch body and pads, checked to stay clear of the lead recesses; the corner's inner boundaries and its north edge are read off the plate silhouette (it stays flush with the switch cells beside it), its east edge off the board outline. Last, the whole hole pattern is shifted `pattern_shift` mm along +X with the outline held in place (0.25 mm by default, an empirical correction for a drift seen on printed plates whose cause is not yet found; see SUGGESTIONS.md), by translating the plate, clipping it solid by solid back to the original silhouette and filling the strips that open on the trailing edges. A `common()` of the whole compound silently drops a cell on FreeCAD 1.0, hence solid by solid. Then `side_shave` mm (0.1 by default) is clipped off the plate's west and east extremes, nothing off north and south. The word `covered` makes the variant without the hard-to-reach key S123 (bottom row, third from the left): before the pattern shift, its cell solid is replaced by a full-thickness prism of the cell's outline, so the cutout is filled in solid while the outline and the contact with the neighbouring cells stay as they are, and a rectangular pit for its diode D123 (measured 1.14 mm tall) is cut 1.6 mm deep into the underside, leaving three print layers over it, since a single 0.2 mm print layer showed through, as it did over the lead recesses. The right half gets the cover through the mirror, after a check that flipped parts sit at exactly the same board positions there (S243, D243). Right before export the solids are fused into one, checked to be a single valid solid of unchanged volume, and the mesh is checked to be closed, manifold and free of self-intersections, so the STL has no internal walls.

`model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_pcb.py` exports a printable replica of the bare board for test-fitting before the fabricated boards arrive: the board's outline face from the STEP, outline moved inward and every drill hole moved outward by `pull_in` (default 0.2 mm, the expected print swelling per side), extruded to the board thickness. FreeCAD's `makeOffset2D` on the whole face would shrink the holes along with the outline, which is why the script offsets the outer wire and each hole wire separately.

`model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_case.py` exports one shell of a two-shell carrying case, `right` for the right half's (the case's own numbers are constants at the top of the script, no key=value overrides). The halves lie sole to sole with their keys outward, and the shells meet at the plane where the soles touch, the seam. Each shell is built in the STEP frame at its tray's x and y and continues its tray's direction from the seam, which lies the tray floor and a `SOLE_THICKNESS_MM` sole below the tray's cavity floor: the left shell opens toward -Z and is turned over for printing, the right one prints as built. From the seam: a tray pocket (the tray's outside, from `tray_shape.py`'s defaults, plus `CASE_CLEARANCE_MM` per side) up to the tray's rim, a step the rim rests on, a key pocket (the tray's outside less `STEP_OVERLAP_MM` per side), the floor, all corners concentric with the tray's. Outside, a rounded rectangle `WALL_MM` beyond the tray pocket's bounding box. The north wall gets a notch from the seam over each tray window, placed by `tray_shape.py` (the USB one sized for an assumed plug grip), the seam face four magnet pockets, and the north and south walls a Velcro tunnel each. Three magnets and the tunnels sit at given board x positions, the fourth magnet halfway between the two notches' facing edges, all centered across the wall between the tray pocket and the outside. The script probes every feature and measures the plastic around each magnet pocket and tunnel with `distToShape` against the pocket, notch and other cutters and the outside's faces other than the seam and floor faces, failing below `MINIMUM_WALL_MM`. `build_case` returns the shell in the STEP frame before it is turned, so a probe can exec the script like the other exports and set the shell against the tray from `export_tray.py`'s `main` (with `export_stl` and `turn_for_printing` replaced in its namespace). One did, for both halves, and found no overlap, the rim flat on the step and 0.3 mm around the tray.

Shared machinery lives in `model/kbdkid4/scripts/board_step.py`: locating the board without hardcoded face indices (by the `PCB` assembly label, a constant in LibrePCB's `stepexport.cpp`, validated geometrically, with a geometry-only fallback), mounting drill detection, STL export with a check that the written mesh is one closed, manifold shell, and the freecadcmd scaffolding. Its module docstring documents the freecadcmd quirks. The solder spool axle's and the camera arm's export scripts import it too, for the STL export and the scaffolding, so a change to it reaches their STLs. `model/kbdkid4/scripts/tray_shape.py` holds what the tray export shares with the case export: which way the tray opens (from how far components protrude on each side of the board), the outline helpers the tray's walls are built from (sharp-corner rebuild, uniform corner fillets), the tray's corner radius, its dimension defaults (gap, wall, floor, depth, standoff height) and where the power switch and USB windows sit along the wall and above the floor, with the wall's outward direction there.

Both halves are exported. The right tray is not a mirror of the left: it is built with the board's other side up (the `right` word), where the right half's switches and sockets are, and turned over afterwards for printing, so it carries no ledge notches (only the left half's sockets reach the ledge); its ledge reliefs (cut beside any underside body within `relief_clearance` of the ledge: the nano, at that half's measured socket height, the socket bodies and the battery connector) follow that half's parts. Behind the left tray's ledge notches the wall's inner face is pocketed so the socket tab tips keep `LEDGE_NOTCH_CLEARANCE_MM` from it, and on both trays a standoff gets a vertical flat facing any of those bodies that comes within `STANDOFF_RELIEF_CLEARANCE_MM` of it. Both USB windows are the 10.5 x 4.5 mm window measured on the soldered left half, with rounded corners and chamfered faces. The right one sits 1.74 mm lower, since that half's nano is on 4.3 mm sockets instead of the left's 2.56 mm. The right plate and the right PCB replica are the left ones mirrored (a printed replica is not its own mirror image the way a bare board is, since the bed side and the top side print differently).

## CI

`.github/workflows/ci.yml` has four kbdkid4 jobs, in parallel with the other projects' jobs: `kbdkid4-tray` exports the tray for both halves (`kbdkid4_v1_Tray_Left.stl`, `kbdkid4_v1_Tray_Right.stl`), `kbdkid4-plate` the plate for both halves, plain and `covered` (`kbdkid4_v1_Plate_Left.stl`, `kbdkid4_v1_Plate_Right.stl`, `kbdkid4_v1_Plate_Left_Covered.stl`, `kbdkid4_v1_Plate_Right_Covered.stl`), `kbdkid4-pcb` the PCB replica for both halves (`kbdkid4_v1_Pcb_Left.stl`, `kbdkid4_v1_Pcb_Right.stl`) and `kbdkid4-case` both case shells (`kbdkid4_v1_Case_Left.stl`, `kbdkid4_v1_Case_Right.stl`). Each job checks out this repository, then Tubbles/kbdkid4 into `external/kbdkid4` (`actions/checkout` with `repository` and `path`), restores or builds the FreeCAD image through `.github/actions/freecad-image`, runs its exports as above (the plate with `/work/resources/kbdkid3-plate-left.FCStd` before the STL) and uploads the STLs as `kbdkid4-tray-stl-<short commit hash>`, `kbdkid4-plate-stl-<short commit hash>`, `kbdkid4-pcb-stl-<short commit hash>` or `kbdkid4-case-stl-<short commit hash>`. The hash is this repository's commit, not kbdkid4's.

## Conventions

- kbdkid4's default board file (the first entry in its `boards/boards.lp`, currently `boards/board_1.1/board.lp`) is the design ground truth (outline polygon vertices, mounting drills). Cross-check what the script detects from the STEP against it rather than trusting either alone. The scripts resolve it through `boards/boards.lp`, so renaming a board directory needs no script change.
- To inspect geometry beyond the exit code, put the probe into the export script so it prints in the CI log: census cylinder-face radii, sample wall thickness with `distToShape`, compare volumes against analytic values. `board_step.py` and `tray_shape.py` import normally (each export script puts its own directory on `sys.path`), but an export script ends in `run_and_exit(main)`, which a plain import would run, ending the interpreter through `os._exit`. A probe that needs another export's geometry execs that script with the trailing line stripped and `__file__` predefined.
- LibrePCB's populated STEP export imports as one `App::Part` per component, the switch package (two 3D models) with a second container nested inside. `Part.getShape` on a component's container returned those switch bodies unplaced in x and y while single-level components came out right; walking each container's tree and multiplying the ancestor placements onto every `Part::Feature` shape places everything correctly. It comes from kbdkid4's STEP job with `(variant default)` instead of `(variant none)`, run from a temporary jobs file, kept untracked at `work/kbdkid4-probes/jobs_step_populated.lp`:

  ```
  (librepcb_jobs
   (job dfaf1cb4-a033-430d-a16f-ffa28a439fd6 (name "STEP Model")
    (type 3d_model)
    (board default)
    (variant default)
    (output "{{PROJECT}}_{{VERSION}}.step")
   )
  )
  ```
