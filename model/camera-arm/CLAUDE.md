# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code working on the camera arm, in `model/camera-arm/`. Read its [README.md](README.md) first (the parts, the hardware, the assembly, where the numbers come from, the CI artifact), since this file does not repeat it. The workshop's rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md). The current design is work item 030, the T-rails and spacer rails 029's.

## The script

`model/camera-arm/scripts/export_camera_arm.py` builds every part from Part primitives, with no STEP input. Its docstring has the sources, the assumptions, the print orientations and the frames. The freecadcmd quirks it works around are in the module docstring of `model/kbdkid4/scripts/board_step.py`.

- The camera's pose is computed, not set: `lens_height()` takes `lens_drop()` off the second arm's lens (8a8eb48's, `second_arm_lens()`, `SECOND_ARM_LENS_X_MM`), `lens_x()` puts the shells' easternmost corner on the Pi case's east edge, and `camera_target()` lies `CAMERA_TARGET_SOUTH_MM` in front of the bed's center at mid travel. The shells' extremes come from the corners of the boxes they are built from (`shells_box_ranges()`), not their bounding box. The `FIRST_ARM_*` constants are the first print's pose, which the second arm's height is worked out from.
- The arm is one bar of the camera bar's section along `arm_path()`: the run on the rail, the riser, the upper leg, the bar. `arm_sections()` carries the bar's section back and the run's section forward through the bends (`carried_through_bend()`), and the riser is a ruled loft between the two, which takes up the twist. `bend_solids()` revolves the two halves of a section split along the bend's axis. `bar_corner_u()` iterates the bar's corner until the riser's back edge and the corner both stand `HEAD_CLEARANCE_MM` off the head's reach, and `riser_x()` stands the riser `RISER_CASE_GAP_MM` off the Pi case's east side. `arm_clip_box()` cuts what bulges behind the run's back face.
- The rail stack is laid out from the Pi mount: the arm's foot (`arm_base_range()`) lies against its back end, and the spacer runs from the upright's bolt (`upright_bolt_front()`) to the foot.
- The arm, the spacer and the shim share `T_RAIL_HALF_PROFILE_MM`. The spacer and shim are cut off at `SLOT_MOUTH_CHAMFER_DEPTH_MM`.
- FreeCAD's `BoundBox` is loose around the arm's clipped curved faces (0.317 mm in 0422d3d), so a part's print offset and the `arm box:` line come from `tight_bound()`, over a tessellated copy. The review page maps the arm's STL back onto the printer from that line.
- The script checks each part is one valid solid with a closed mesh, probes the T-rails, the spacer and shim rails (nothing in the slot's mouth chamfer) and the bar's insert bores, checks the shells, the board model, the arm, the rail, the spacer, the Pi mount and the camera's field of view do not overlap, checks a 5 mm straight driver reaches the ear screws past the front shell, lists planar faces facing down past 45 degrees in the print orientation, and measures the clearances to the upright, the gantry's right end, the bed and knobs over the travel, the print volume, the print head's reach, the Pi case and the cable's corridor, and how much of the print area the lens sees at each bed Y position, plain and with the upright, the gantry end and the X beam at their lowest as occluders. The distance to the Pi case slid off its keyholes is printed, not checked.
- The face check reads planar faces only: curved ones, such as the insert bores' ceilings and the riser's twisted faces, are not listed.
