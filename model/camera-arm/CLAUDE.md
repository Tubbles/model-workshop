# CLAUDE.md

Guidance for Claude Code working on the camera arm, in `model/camera-arm/`. Read its [README.md](README.md) first (the parts, the hardware, the assembly, where the numbers come from, the CI artifact), since this file does not repeat it. The workshop's rules are in the root [CLAUDE.md](../../CLAUDE.md). The current design is work items 030, 031 (nothing east of the Pi case) and 032 (the riser behind the Pi case's keyhole slide), the T-rails and spacer rails 029's.

## The script

`model/camera-arm/scripts/export_camera_arm.py` builds every part from Part primitives, with no STEP input. Its docstring has the sources, the assumptions, the print orientations and the frames. The freecadcmd quirks it works around are in the module docstring of `model/kbdkid4/scripts/board_step.py`.

- The camera's pose is computed, not set: `lens_height()` takes `lens_drop()` off the second arm's lens (8a8eb48's, `second_arm_lens()`, `SECOND_ARM_LENS_X_MM`), `lens_x()` iterates until `bar_east_for()`, the bar's bottom back edge at the case's north end, lies on the Pi case's east edge, and `camera_target()` lies `CAMERA_TARGET_SOUTH_MM` in front of the bed's center at mid travel. The shells' extremes come from the corners of the boxes they are built from (`shells_box_ranges()`), not their bounding box. The `FIRST_ARM_*` constants are the first print's pose, which the second arm's height is worked out from.
- The arm is one bar of the camera bar's section along `arm_path()`: the run on the rail, a leg leaning back to a waist at the Pi case's rearmost corner's height, the plumb riser at `riser_x()` (its east face on the Pi case's edge) and `riser_distance()` (`PI_CASE_CLEARANCE_MM` behind the case slid off its keyholes), the bend forward into the bar, a straight stretch past the riser's front face, a ruled loft to the case's north end (`bar_north_u()`) and the bar under the case. `arm_sections()` carries the run's section up and forward (two bends at the run's corner keep its 15 mm side along x), and the loft takes up the twist and the step to the case's section. The loft starts past the riser's front face because one starting inside the riser met it in nearly tangent faces and left an invalid solid (40b19b9). `bend_solids()` revolves the two halves of a section split along the bend's axis. Nothing clips the bar: the riser's back face is the print's bed contact.
- The head's reach ends `HEAD_REACH_PAST_RAIL_MM` past the rail's outer side, well west of the riser and the bar, so it shapes nothing of the arm. Before 032 it was assumed to run far east, and the riser leaned around it.
- The rail stack is laid out from the Pi mount: the arm's foot (`arm_base_range()`) lies against its back end, and the spacer runs from the upright's bolt (`upright_bolt_front()`) to the foot.
- The arm, the spacer and the shim share `T_RAIL_HALF_PROFILE_MM`. The spacer and shim are cut off at `SLOT_MOUTH_CHAMFER_DEPTH_MM`.
- FreeCAD's `BoundBox` is loose around curved faces (0.317 mm around 0422d3d's clipped arm), so a part's print offset, the `arm box:` line and the east check come from `tight_bound()`, over a tessellated copy. The review page maps the arm's STL back onto the printer from that line.
- The script checks each part is one valid solid with a closed mesh, probes the T-rails, the spacer and shim rails (nothing in the slot's mouth chamfer) and the bar's insert bores, checks the shells, the board model, the arm, the rail, the spacer, the Pi mount and the camera's field of view do not overlap, checks nothing of the arm or the shells lies east of the Pi case's east edge (`check_east_of_pi_case()`), checks a 5 mm straight driver reaches the ear screws past the front shell, lists planar faces facing down past 45 degrees in the print orientation, and measures the clearances to the upright, the gantry's right end, the bed and knobs over the travel, the print volume, the print head's reach, the Pi case and the cable's corridor, and how much of the print area the lens sees at each bed Y position, plain and with the upright, the gantry end and the X beam at their lowest as occluders. The Pi case slid `PI_CASE_SLIDE_OFF_MM` up its mount (`pi_case_slid_off()`) is a keep-out like the case itself, so the export fails if the arm comes nearer it than `PI_CASE_CLEARANCE_MM`. The riser stands at exactly that distance by construction.
- The face check reads planar faces only: curved ones, such as the insert bores' ceilings and the bar's twisted loft, are not listed.
