# 003 Tray shell and joint

Status: implemented

`build_tray()` in `model/kbdkid5/scripts/tray_shape.py`, kbdkid4's tray pipeline on the layout instead of a STEP: walls, floor, ledge and standoffs (bored for M2 heat-set inserts) on the layout's outline, the sockets' cuts, and the slanted joint (block, magnet slots open toward the cavity with the wall cut back over them, dowel pin bosses with flat-topped teardrop holes, snug on the left half and slip with a lead-in on the right, fills closing the crevices beside the bosses, the joint face's bottom chamfer, and the pin parking hole with its magnet seat and boss). Every printed fit allows for the print's 0.2 swelling per side, the PCB mock's `pull_in`. The ledge cut-aways `layout.py` declares for the parts in the tray are cut, and 004 mounts the parts. `model/kbdkid5/scripts/export_tray.py` exports either half, checks it against DESIGN.md's figures, transcribed in the script, and joined with the other half, and writes one closed mesh. Both halves are in CI as the `tray-stl` artifact. The builder sits in `tray_shape.py` beside the outline helpers, as `plate_shape.py` and `pcb_shape.py` hold theirs, rather than in a module of its own.

The sockets' cuts (DESIGN.md, "Tray") come from `model/kbdkid5/scripts/layout.py`, which applies kbdkid4's rules to the sockets it names for each half: `all_socket_tray_cuts(right)` gives the tab notches, the wall pockets behind them and the ledge reliefs as boxes in an outline edge's frame (`tray_cut_polygon()` in plan, a z range, `cuts_wall` set on the pockets), and `all_socket_standoff_flats(right)` the standoff flats. kbdkid4 measured its cuts along the straight stretches between the outline's corner rounds. These use the sharp edges. As on kbdkid4, every cut but the pockets is clipped to the cavity, so it never reaches the wall, and a cut that finds nothing left to remove (a tab's relief inside its notch) is skipped. `model/kbdkid5/scripts/check_layout.py` checks the sockets against the tray with these cuts, and the export cuts exactly them.

The right half's switch footprints sit unmirrored at the mirrored key centers (DESIGN.md, "Frame and units", now a constraint on the PCB design), so its sockets reach toward the slant. The right tray is built in the left half's frame with its own socket cuts and then mirrored: a tab notch and wall pocket for S163 at the thumb's outer edge instead of column 0's, the bottom row's ledge reliefs, and no standoff flats (no socket comes within 1.0 of a standoff, closest 1.391). With the upper dowel pin at 55.5 along the slant, the right half's S150 socket overlapped its boss by 1.641. The pins moved to 3.5 and 59.0, the farthest apart the joint face allows, and the four magnets between them (DESIGN.md, "The joint", and doc/log/2026-10-17.md). check_layout.py now runs every socket check, the joint's and the parking hole's on both halves, and failed on the old joint.
