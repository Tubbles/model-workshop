# Suggestions

Ideas noticed during work, not yet asked for. Strike or promote as you see fit.

## Workshop

- One shared FreeCAD scaffolding module. `model/kbdkid4/scripts/board_step.py` and `model/kbdkid5/scripts/freecad_common.py` both carry the freecadcmd scaffolding and the STL export (their module docstrings), the second a port of the first, and `model/solder-spool-axle/` and `model/camera-arm/` reach into kbdkid4's directory for it through their `sys.path` line. A module beside `docker/` would let every project import one copy. Not done in the move (2026-10-21), whose promise was byte-identical STLs with only path changes, and the two copies' STL export settings would have to be compared first.
- The kbdkid4 artifacts name only this repository's commit. Which kbdkid4 CI run and commit gave the STEP and the board files is printed in the job log by `.github/actions/kbdkid4-board`, but not in the download, so two downloads with the same name can hold different parts after a board change in kbdkid4. Writing the kbdkid4 commit into the artifact name or into a small text file inside it would make a download say which board it was built from. Not done because the user asked only for the STEP to come from kbdkid4's CI.

## kbdkid5

- A fit test strip: a few short bosses, each with a horizontal flat-topped teardrop hole of a different diameter as modelled (say 4.3 to 4.7 in 0.05 steps), printed in the tray's orientation, to settle the snug (left) and slip (right) pin fits before printing two whole trays. The magnet slots could go on it too. `tray_shape.cut_teardrop_hole()` already makes the hole.
- A coupon like the nice!nano bay test strip (work item 006) for the north wall's three small mounts (the cradle with its lever slot, the reset back wall with its pin hole, the battery pocket), whose fits work item 004 lists as not printed.
- Socket strip pin holes past 3.54 (`socket_test_shape.py`, DESIGN.md "Hand-wired frame test", Pocket strip): merge the tab recess into the pin hole on purpose instead of leaving a web under `RECESS_HOLE_CLEARANCE_MM` (export_socket_test.py). The south tab's recess starts at the body's end, 2.07 from the pin hole at footprint (-5.95, 0.0), so the web is 0.12 at 3.9 and the two merge at 4.14. A web that thin as modelled prints as a sliver, and the recess and the hole belong to the same contact, so a merge costs nothing. The frame's pin holes are settled at 3.2 (DESIGN.md, Hole strip), so this matters only for a strip that still wants holes past 3.54.
- The tray's socket cover could take the real step, which its lobes miss (DESIGN.md "Hand-wired frame test", Frame cells): `layout.HOTSWAP_SOCKET_LOBES_MM` meet at footprint-local y 2.25, where the body steps at 2.68 (`layout.HOTSWAP_SOCKET_OUTLINE_MM`). Lobes meeting at 2.68, with the fills redrawn to match, would still cover the body. Whether any of the tray's socket cuts or `check_layout.py`'s socket figures is set by that corner has not been checked. Both trays, the nice!nano test bay and `check_layout.py`'s figures would have to be compared before and after.
- A next backing's pads could follow the socket's outline (moved in as the walls are moved out) instead of the cover's lobe rectangles (`PAD_LOBE_RECTANGLES_MM`), which bring the walls close to the pads (DESIGN.md "Hand-wired frame test", Backing).

## kbdkid4

- **Remaining tight spot between the populated board and the tray.** Measured 2026-10-12 with FreeCAD on LibrePCB's populated STEP export against the trays `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_tray.py` builds, board resting on the standoffs (probe in `work/kbdkid4-probes/check_fit.py`, untracked, local only). The ledge reliefs, the wall pockets behind the ledge notches and the standoff flats keep every other underside body at least 0.5 mm from the tray (the nice!nano aside, whose package model the probe uses is known to be wrong), so what is left: the power switch body sits in its wall pocket 0.5 mm from the pocket's ends, and 0.89 mm from the pocket's back by the package model (less on the assembled print, doc/components.md section 11). Measuring the printed cavity between the ledge faces (design 133.42 mm at the board's full width) would size the swelling to design against.
- **Find the cause of the printed plate's hole drift**, which `pattern_shift` in `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_plate.py` corrects empirically (doc/print-model.md: the plate hole pattern row in Measurements, and what was checked in Unmeasured or inferred). Measure: the printed plate's width (design 135.05 mm for the plates printed so far, 134.85 mm since the side shave of 2026-10-10) and the span from the left edge of the bottom-left cutout to the right edge of the thumb cutout (design 128.4 mm), the PCB's two bottom mounting holes centre to centre (design 95.57 mm), and the printed tray's outer width (design 137.02 mm).
- **The power switch lever is hard to reach with the case closed.** In `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_case.py` the switch notch is 12 mm wide and cuts straight through the 7.16 mm thick north wall, so the lever sits about 7.5 mm in (wall plus the 0.3 mm clearance): a fingernail or a pen tip reaches it, a fingertip does not. Flaring the notch's outer edge would let a finger in. Not done in the first version (2026-10-16) because nobody has handled a printed case yet.
- **An unmerged plate change on kbdkid4.** The branch `widen-cones` on Tubbles/kbdkid4 (kbdkid4 8fd2b0d, 2026-10-11, the user's) sets `LEAD_CONE_BASE_DIAMETER_MM` in `export_plate.py` from 2.5 to 2.6. It was never merged and has no pull request. `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_plate.py` here has main's 2.5, and the branch stays on kbdkid4.
