# Suggestions

Ideas noticed during work, not yet asked for. Strike or promote as you see fit.

## kbdkid5

- A fit test strip: a few short bosses, each with a horizontal flat-topped teardrop hole of a different diameter as modelled (say 4.3 to 4.7 in 0.05 steps), printed in the tray's orientation, to settle the snug (left) and slip (right) pin fits before printing two whole trays. The magnet slots could go on it too. `tray_shape.cut_teardrop_hole()` already makes the hole.
- A coupon like the nice!nano bay test strip (work item 006) for the north wall's three small mounts (the cradle with its lever slot, the reset back wall with its pin hole, the battery pocket), all snug fits on figures from drawings, not calipers.
- The battery header's hold against the plug's pull: with the header in hand, look for a step on its body behind the plug's housing that a pocket lip (`battery_shoulder`) could catch. Otherwise a printed strap over the pocket's top, across the header's back half, or glue.
- Socket strip pin holes past 3.54 (`socket_test_shape.py`): merge the tab recess into the pin hole on purpose instead of leaving a web under `RECESS_HOLE_CLEARANCE_MM` 0.3 (export_socket_test.py). The south tab's recess starts at the body's end, 2.07 from the pin hole at footprint (-5.95, 0.0), so it keeps 0.37 from the 3.4 hole, 0.32 from the 3.5 one, 0.12 at 3.9 and merges at 4.14. A web that thin as modelled prints as a sliver, and the recess and the hole belong to the same contact, so a merge costs nothing. The hole strip settled the frame's pin holes at 3.2 (doc/log/2026-10-18.md), so this matters only for a strip that still wants holes past 3.54.
- The tray's socket cover could take the real step: `layout.HOTSWAP_SOCKET_LOBES_MM` meet at footprint-local y 2.25, where the body steps at 2.68 (`layout.HOTSWAP_SOCKET_OUTLINE_MM`), so the second lobe's rectangle reaches 0.43 past the body along the first lobe's side, and more toward its east face, where the body ends in a 0.75 round. Lobes meeting at 2.68, with the fills redrawn to match, would still cover the body. Whether any of the tray's socket cuts or `check_layout.py`'s socket figures is set by that corner has not been checked. Both trays, the nice!nano test bay and `check_layout.py`'s figures would have to be compared before and after.
- A next backing's pads could follow the socket's outline (moved in as the walls are moved out) instead of the cover's lobe rectangles (`PAD_LOBE_RECTANGLES_MM`): lobe B's pad's corner by the 1.5 fill lies 0.534 inside the body, lobe A's pad 0.813 at its nearest, so the walls come within 0.135 of the pads across as printed under the pocket strip's cell 1.

## kbdkid4

- **Remaining tight spot between the populated board and the tray.** Measured 2026-10-12 with FreeCAD on LibrePCB's populated STEP export against the trays `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_tray.py` builds, board resting on the standoffs (probe in `work/kbdkid4-probes/check_fit.py`, untracked, local only). The ledge reliefs, the wall pockets behind the ledge notches and the standoff flats keep every other underside body at least 0.5 mm from the tray (the nice!nano aside, whose package model the probe uses is known to be wrong), so what is left: the power switch body sits in its wall pocket 0.5 mm from the pocket's ends, and 0.89 mm from the pocket's back by the package model (0.73 mm by the 2026-10-12 measurement on the assembled print, which has the body 0.07 mm past the board edge where the model has it 0.09 mm inside). Measuring the printed cavity between the ledge faces (design 133.42 mm at the board's full width) would size the swelling to design against.
- **The printed plate's holes drift about 1 mm toward the nice!nano side over the plate's width; the cause is not found.** Checked 2026-10-09: kbdkid4's `boards/board_1.1/board.lp` has every switch pitch at exactly 19.05 mm, kbdkid4's committed Excellon files put the 3.4 mm switch drills and the 2.4 mm mounting drills at the same positions, the kbdkid3 plate model's cutouts match the switch grid to 0.0005 mm, the exported plate STL has every cutout, screw hole and the reset hole at its design x within 0.08 mm, and the tray STL's standoff bores sit at the mounting drills to 0.001 mm. Both parts were printed unscaled. A 1 mm drift at 125 mm is 0.8 %, the size of a print scale or shrinkage error, but a plate shrunk that much should sit loose in the tray, which it does not. `pattern_shift` in `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_plate.py` corrects it empirically: a 0.5 mm shift came out offset about as far the other way (printed 2026-10-10; a linear 1 mm drift would have left both ends 0.5 mm off in opposite directions instead), so the default is 0.25 mm now. To find the cause, measure: the printed plate's width (design 135.05 mm for the plates printed so far, 134.85 mm since the side shave of 2026-10-10) and the span from the left edge of the bottom-left cutout to the right edge of the thumb cutout (design 128.4 mm), the PCB's two bottom mounting holes centre to centre (design 95.57 mm), and the printed tray's outer width (design 137.02 mm).
- **The power switch lever is hard to reach with the case closed.** In `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_case.py` the switch notch is 12 mm wide and cuts straight through the 7.16 mm thick north wall, so the lever sits about 7.5 mm in (wall plus the 0.3 mm clearance): a fingernail or a pen tip reaches it, a fingertip does not. Flaring the notch's outer edge would let a finger in. Not done in the first version (2026-10-16) because nobody has handled a printed case yet.
- **An unmerged plate change on kbdkid4.** The branch `widen-cones` on Tubbles/kbdkid4 (8fd2b0d, 2026-10-11, the user's) sets `LEAD_CONE_BASE_DIAMETER_MM` in `export_plate.py` from 2.5 to 2.6. It was never merged and has no pull request. `model/kbdkid4/scripts/export_plate.py` here has main's 2.5, and the branch stays on kbdkid4.
